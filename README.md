# How to become a part of the conversation
This is **a thinking-in-public repo, not a finished product** and it’s **certainly not a representation of my employer’s views** or those of really anyone else.

I do post shortened versions to LinkedIn, so happy to chat in comments there or through the repo here.

One of my goals is to be transparent in my thinking and open to criticism from others, so you’re welcome to open an [issue](https://docs.github.com/en/issues/tracking-your-work-with-issues/using-issues/creating-an-issue) or submit a [pull request](https://docs.github.com/en/pull-requests/collaborating-with-pull-requests/proposing-changes-to-your-work-with-pull-requests/creating-a-pull-request) for:
- Factual corrections — **did I get it wrong?** Was I right before, but the facts on the ground have changed? Please **let me know**!
- Clarifications — if **you think there’s a clearer way to explain something**, I’d be interested to hear your thoughts.
- Extensions — did **I stop short of what you'd like to see**? Let me know, especially if you have a proposal for what to expand. Maybe that’ll be my next essay.

# Why the name “Maker of Windows"?
Why *Maker of Windows*? It's a family thing. **Fenstermacher’s literal translation** is *window maker*, as my ancestors made stained-glass windows for churches in present-day Germany, in [the Palatinate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electoral_Palatinate). I’ve also been intrigued by a lineage that carries through to my making virtual windows today (and I do still love [birch beer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birch_beer) and [shoofly pie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoofly_pie) and will shout out [Schuykill County, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schuylkill_County,_Pennsylvania) til the day I die.)

So why not simply *Window Maker*? Fewer name collisions. And for me, the emphasis is on the making. But I do also like that I might give someone else a new way to see things and what is a window, but a way to see things from a new perspective?
